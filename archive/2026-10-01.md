# Daily Dev Digest — 2026-10-01

> Theme: Open weights you can actually run, train against and point at a screen — compressed to fit, shipped with the gym, and able to see.

## Picks

### Ternary Bonsai 2 27B — PrismML's 27B reasoning model squeezed to ~8.5 GB
Apache-2.0 model derived from Qwen3.8-27B, announced September 17, with ternary-compressed weights of roughly 8.5 GB. PrismML says it keeps 98.2% of the full-precision model's average score across its own 14 thinking-mode benchmarks, with gains in agentic coding and long-horizon tool use. The retention figure is the vendor's own, but the direction matters: a 27B reasoner that fits consumer hardware.

### MiMo-V2.6 — Xiaomi's open-weight Pro/Flash/9B family, with the RL environments included
Released in late September, with Flash (309B total / 15B active MoE, 1M-token context, text/image/video/audio input) published on Hugging Face under MIT. Beyond weights, Xiaomi open-sourced more than 7,000 RL environments for software development, vulnerability reproduction and web work, plus a framework to drive them. Shipping the training gym next to the model is the unusual part.

### Ling 3.0 Flash VL — inclusionAI's MoE that adds vision to an agent-oriented base
Released September 10: builds on Ling 3.0 Flash (124B total / 5.5B active) with native image and video understanding and visual-agent capabilities, and supports tool calling and structured output. At 5.5B active parameters it is aimed at agents that have to look at a screen, not just read text.
