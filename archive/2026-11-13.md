# Daily Dev Digest — 2026-11-13

> Theme: Agents get sharper on both sides of the firewall — attackers run swarms, vendors gate cyber models, and a small mask beats full fine-tuning.

## Picks

### Deletion Tuning — adapt an LLM by zeroing under 0.05% of its weights
A new arXiv paper learns a binary mask over the gate projections of a pretrained model instead of updating weights. The authors report it matches full fine-tuning across RL with verifiable rewards, SFT and on-policy distillation, with a mask hundreds to thousands of times smaller than an FFT checkpoint. They also report less forgetting under RL than FFT, LoRA or random sparse tuning. Early (v1, NeurIPS 2026 submission), so wait for replication.

### Dream's report on an eight-agent hacking campaign — state-linked intrusion built on Hermes and OpenClaw
Dream, via Recorded Future and GBHackers, describes a July 2026 campaign where waves of up to eight sub-agents ran recon, password spraying with CAPTCHA solving, and JWT "alg: none" abuse, reportedly yielding 85 cracked credentials and 2,500+ records. It is a concrete case of off-the-shelf agent frameworks doing multi-step intrusion, with refusals bypassed by "authorized pentest" framing. Dream says only "Asia"; the headline's Taiwan attribution is GBHackers' wording.

### GPT-6 Cyber — OpenAI's fourth cyber model of the year, limited to approved customers
Released Nov 5 for Daybreak Red customers, with a "Critical" cybersecurity rating under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework. OpenAI's own figures show 48.5% on ExploitGym vs 32.1% for GPT-5.6 Cyber. It lands the same week as the agent-intrusion report: capability and gating are moving together. Benchmarks are vendor-reported.

### PostgreSQL 19 — release candidate stage, GA planned for Nov 19
PostgreSQL's release notes list 19 RC 2 dated 2026-11-12, alongside minor releases for 14–18. Earlier betas highlighted parallel autovacuum workers, the REPACK command with CONCURRENTLY, and native SQL/PGQ graph queries, though EnterpriseDB notes some features were reverted. GA date is a plan, not a promise. Worth testing upgrades now.
