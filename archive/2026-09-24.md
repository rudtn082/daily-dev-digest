# Daily Dev Digest — 2026-09-24

> Theme: Skills, launchers and voices you can own — agent know-how packaged as files, and cloud apps swapped for local ones.

## Picks

### security-audit-skill — Cloudflare's vulnerability-hunting skill for AI agents
Cloudflare open-sourced a multi-phase security auditing skill that agents can load. It hit #1 on Hacker News and landed on Trendshift's weekly board despite a repo that sat quiet after its June creation. A big infra vendor shipping its audit playbook as a portable skill is a signal that skills are becoming the unit of shared expertise.

### tinycast — a Swift macOS launcher that runs Raycast extensions without a Raycast account
Open-source launcher that runs Raycast extensions natively. It took off right after Raycast moved to usage-based credit pricing on September 10, a textbook case of a pricing change creating a free alternative overnight.

### hypit — turn viral videos into editable code, not static renders
Video generation where the output is code you can edit instead of a baked render. It was #2 on Trendshift's weekly board in the Sept 14–18 window, helped by launch partnerships. Worth watching, with the caveat that part of the climb was coordinated promotion.

### VoiceBox — a local AI voice studio that also gives MCP agents a voice
Runs on Mac, Windows and Linux; clones voices from a few seconds of audio, generates speech in 23 languages across seven TTS engines, and exposes voice to MCP-capable agents. Local-first voice is the cheap, private answer to expensive opaque cloud TTS.
