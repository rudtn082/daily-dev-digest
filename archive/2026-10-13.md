# Daily Dev Digest — 2026-10-13

> Theme: Free to use, free to host — AI vuln scans for maintainers, a European open-weight MoE, and a self-hostable Canva alternative.

## Picks

### OSS Scanner — Anthropic's free, opt-in vulnerability scans for open-source projects
Maintainers enroll by opening a pull request to the anthropics/oss-scanner repo with a project.yaml, and get periodic security scans by Anthropic's strongest models at no cost. Launched Oct 8. The catch, per press coverage: reports are fully model-generated with no human triage first, and Anthropic warns some findings may be invalid. Worth watching because it shifts AI bug-hunting from a lab exercise to a service maintainers can sign up for, and it will test how well maintainers cope with unvetted reports.

### Kolibri — Aleph Alpha's 78B German/English MoE with Apache 2.0 weights
Released Oct 3: about 78B total parameters with roughly 3.5B active per token, text-only, aimed at German and English and at self-hosting in regulated sectors. Weights are on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0, about 78 GB in FP8, so one H200 or B200 class GPU is the suggested floor. Benchmarks come from the vendor, and one review says it trails Qwen3.6 35B-A3B on some knowledge and tool-use tests, so check the model card. Interesting as a sovereign-hosting option from a European lab rather than another US or Chinese release.

### Doory — an open-source, self-hostable Canva alternative
A browser design editor for posters, social posts, presentations and resumes, with templates, animations, real-time co-editing and export to PNG, PDF, SVG, MP4 and GIF. Stack is React 19 + Fabric.js + Yjs on the front, Django + PostgreSQL behind, run via Docker Compose. It was on the Hacker News front page on Oct 12. Note the license is Attribution Assurance: commercial use is allowed but the author credit must be shown in the running app.
