# Daily Dev Digest — 2026-11-21

> Theme: Boring infrastructure ships, big open weights land, and agents move into Slack

## Picks

### PostgreSQL 19 — GA, with online table repacking
The PostgreSQL project announced the PostgreSQL 19 release on Nov 19, after the release-candidate stage covered earlier this month. Headline items in the announcement: `REPACK (CONCURRENTLY)` to reclaim table space without blocking reads or writes, a `pg_plan_advice` extension for guiding the planner, parallel-worker index processing in autovacuum, and `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT DO SELECT`. It is on the radar because it was a top HN thread on Nov 20 and the repack feature removes a long-standing reason to reach for external tools. Secondary sources disagree on whether SQL/PGQ graph queries made the cut; check the official notes before planning around them.

### Hy4 Flash — Tencent's open-weight follow-up to the Hy4 preview
On Nov 18 Tencent released three checkpoints: Hy4 Flash, Hy4 Flash-FP8 and Hy4 Flash Base, on GitHub, Hugging Face, ModelScope and GitCode. The model card describes 220B total parameters with about 12B active per token, a much smaller footprint than the 770B Hy4 preview. It closes the "weights in the coming days" loop from the Nov 4 announcement. The license looks custom rather than Apache 2.0, with reported limits for very large services; secondary sources only, so read the LICENSE file first.

### OpenComputer — open-source cloud computers for agents, now with Slack entry points
OpenComputer (repo listed as diggerhq/opencomputer) positions itself as long-running cloud infrastructure for AI agents, "real computers, not sandboxes." It surfaced on Hacker News on Nov 20 and a write-up describes durable agent sessions you can start and steer from a Slack thread, with one Slack app per agent per workspace. It matters as another sign that agent runtimes are moving to where teams already talk. Details come from a secondary article and an HN digest; verify against the repo and docs, including how credentials are scoped.
