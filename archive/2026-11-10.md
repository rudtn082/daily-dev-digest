# Daily Dev Digest — 2026-11-10

> Theme: Frontier up, price down — a flagship refresh, a cheap agent workhorse, and an open-source CAD that hit a nerve.

## Picks

### Claude Fable 5.2 — Anthropic's flagship refresh, plus a gated Mythos 5.2
Anthropic says Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 are the same underlying model, upgraded in coding, reasoning and vision, and a step above Opus 5.5 on almost everything it evaluated. Outputs are up to 30% faster than 5.1; list price is $8 / $40 per million input / output tokens, with an optional fast mode at 2.5x speed for $16 / $80. Fable has cyber and biology safeguards and is open to all users; Mythos is limited to verification-program organizations. All capability claims are Anthropic's own, and Hacker News commenters were already arguing about whether benchmark scores track real engineering work.

### Muse Ember — Meta's smallest model, built for the work agents repeat all day
Meta pitches Ember at tool calls, routing, extraction and long-document parsing, not frontier reasoning. It claims to match Claude Haiku 5.5's best Artificial Analysis score at about a third of the cost, at $0.10 / $0.50 per million tokens (prompts up to 200K) with a 1M-token context. Availability is the Meta Model API, Muse Code, Google Cloud and Oracle Cloud as `muse-ember-1.0`; the page does not mention open weights. Benchmarks are Meta's own or Artificial Analysis as quoted by Meta, so test on your own workload.

### KetchUp — an open-source SketchUp alternative in Rust and WebGPU
A browser-based 3D modeling app that climbed past 700 points on Hacker News. Commenters tie the interest to long-time SketchUp users looking for alternatives after Trimble's licensing changes. Early and young: one commenter notes the latest release renamed it, and another points out an unrelated tool with the same name. The linked page does not state a license in what I could read, so check the repo before depending on it.
